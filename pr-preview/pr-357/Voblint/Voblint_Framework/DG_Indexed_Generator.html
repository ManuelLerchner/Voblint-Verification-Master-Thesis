section ‹Keyed equation generators›

text ‹
  Builds a strategy-tree equation system over a CFG from supplied
  edge/combine/extra program hooks, where every unknown carries an extra ‹'k›
  key alongside its node -- context, activation, or any other routing tag a
  caller wants the generator to thread through. Specifications, routing
  conventions, and analysis-specific representations are instantiated above
  this layer; the generator itself never sees one.
  The ten parameters, once:

  ▪ ‹pred_sel› --- which intra predecessors a node folds, each at its address.
  ▪ ‹site_sel› --- which call sites return at a node.
  ▪ ‹route› --- the callee context a call site selects, threaded to ‹cmb› and
    ‹extra› and never inspected here.
  ▪ ‹it› --- the program for one intra edge.
  ▪ ‹cmb› --- the program for one call returning at this node.
  ▪ ‹extra› --- the routing protocol's own per-node programs, such as an
    activation-seed read.
  ▪ ‹buffer_key_at› --- the node-owned global buffer address for a context.
  ▪ ‹bot0› --- the local value a node starts its fold from.
  ▪ ‹d⇩0› --- the local value injected at the CFG entry.
  ▪ ‹g⇩0› --- the global value published at the CFG entry.

  Two encodings, and they are not peers. ‹routed_node_rhs› is the direct,
  reference one: each publishing hook emits its own \<^const>‹Side›, keyed
  and folded independently. ‹routed_node_rhs_buffered› is what production
  runs --- every executable analysis in this development reaches the solver
  through it. It differs deliberately: contributions return their global part
  in the answer, so the node's own ‹buffer_key_at c› is published once, after the whole
  fold has been evaluated, and the enclosing \<^const>‹buffer_sides› then
  consolidates ∗‹every› key it names --- activation seeds included --- into
  one flush apiece. The flush comes before the next local read at a node where
  at most one call returns, and at the answer elsewhere.

  ‹routed_node_rhs_buffered_correspondence› relates the two declaratively:
  equal answer, publications and dependencies at a fixed valuation, under
  explicit per-hook hypotheses. That is all it says. Nothing here claims the
  two run the same: equal observations at each valuation leave the iteration
  order free, and where the first occurrence of a key falls decides which
  update the solver widens against. Deferring the node-global publication past
  the contributions is therefore a scheduling decision, not an encoding
  detail, and swapping the encodings is a question for the executable
  regression corpus rather than for these theorems.
›

subsection ‹The heterogeneous seeded keyed generator›

text ‹
  The one context-generic generator.  Every program it folds comes from a hook, so
  a monovariant and a context-sensitive analysis are both instances, and the
  generator itself never sees a specification:

  ▪ ‹pred_sel g v ctx› selects the intra predecessors folded as Answers into a node,
    each paired with the ∗‹address› in the solver's valuation space that carries its
    value.  ‹intra_predecessor_addr_list› over \<^const>‹intra› addresses every
    predecessor at its own ‹(pp, 'c)› local unknown. Callee entries are not
    predecessors: every routed instance reaches them through published seeds.
  ▪ ‹site_sel g v› selects the call sites whose calls return at a node.
    \<^const>‹call_site_list› reads them off \<^const>‹calls›; an executable
    instance may answer from an index built once per graph, since the generator
    only folds the list.
  ▪ ‹it c src a› is the intra edge program itself -- an instantiator supplies the
    compiled right-hand side for one edge action at one source address, e.g.
    a spec's compiled ‹dg_spec_edge_program› at the routed key. Which unknowns it
    reads and which keys it publishes are its own business; the generator makes
    no assumption about either.
  ▪ ‹cmb› is the procedure-return combine program (already fully abstract: the
    context-sensitive instance reads the callee exit under the routed context).
  ▪ ‹extra c v› supplies additional per-node programs folded after the intra and
    combine programs.  Their Answers add to the node's local accumulator and their
    \<^const>‹Side› effects are collected --- this is where a frame-entry seed
    ∗‹read› (a \<^const>‹QueryG› of the incoming seed slot) and the caller-side
    call-entry ∗‹publication› (a routed \<^const>‹Side› to the callee seed
    slot) live.  Every routed instance supplies one, the context-insensitive
    one included: seeding is what activates a callee at all, so no analysis
    reaching a call leaves this hook empty.
›

text ‹The standard predecessor selection: every intra predecessor is carried by its
  own ‹(pp, 'c)› local unknown, so its address is \<^const>‹Inl› of that pair.›

definition intra_predecessor_addr_list ::
  "(var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ ((pp × 'c + 'k) × (var_id, exp) gedge_action) list"
where
  "intra_predecessor_addr_list g v ctx =
     map (λ(u, a). (Inl (u, ctx), a)) (intra_predecessor_list g v)"

text ‹
  Everything that flows into one unknown, in one list: the intra edges
  entering ‹v›, the calls returning at it, and whatever the routing protocol
  adds there. Both generators fold exactly this list --- the buffered one at
  its own hooks --- and every bound, correspondence and monotonicity statement
  below is about it, so it is named once rather than spelled out at each.

  The three membership rules are how a per-hook hypothesis reaches a selected
  contribution. They stay untagged: the list is a concatenation of two maps
  and a hook's own list, so a membership goal is decided by ‹auto› from the
  definition, and tagging them would put three rules in front of every
  \<^const>‹set› membership goal in scope.
›

definition routed_contribution_programs ::
  "((var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ ((pp × 'c + 'k) × (var_id, exp) gedge_action) list)
   ⇒ ((var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ (pp × (var_id, exp) gcall_action) list)
   ⇒ (pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c)
   ⇒ ('c ⇒ pp × 'c + 'k ⇒ (var_id, exp) gedge_action
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot,
                           'h::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) dg_state,
                          ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program)
   ⇒ ((pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ pp ⇒ pp
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program)
   ⇒ ((pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ pp
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program list)
   ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ 'c ⇒ pp
   ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program list"
where
  "routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v =
     map (λ(src, a). it ctx src a) (pred_sel g v ctx)
     @ map (λ(cc, ca). cmb route ctx ca cc v) (site_sel g v)
     @ extra route ctx v"

text ‹
  Every contribution runs its continuation once, given that each hook does.
  The fold's observation lemmas all carry that side condition, and this is
  where a generator instance discharges it: the hooks a context policy
  supplies are built from \<^const>‹sp_return›, the reads and \<^const>‹sp_publish›,
  whose closure lemmas fire by ‹simp›.
›

lemma routed_contribution_programs_wf:
  assumes "⋀c' src a. sp_wf (it c' src a)"
    and "⋀c' ca cc ex. sp_wf (cmb route c' ca cc ex)"
    and "⋀c' w p. p ∈ set (extra route c' w) ⟹ sp_wf p"
  shows "∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v).
           sp_wf p"
  using assms by (auto simp: routed_contribution_programs_def)

lemma routed_contribution_programs_combineI:
  "(cc, ca) ∈ set (site_sel g v)
   ⟹ cmb route ctx ca cc v
         ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v)"
  by (force simp: routed_contribution_programs_def)

lemma routed_contribution_programs_extraI:
  "t ∈ set (extra route ctx v)
   ⟹ t ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v)"
  by (simp add: routed_contribution_programs_def)

lemma routed_contribution_programsE:
  assumes "t ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v)"
  obtains src a where "(src, a) ∈ set (pred_sel g v ctx)" "t = it ctx src a"
    | cc ca where "(cc, ca) ∈ set (site_sel g v)" "t = cmb route ctx ca cc v"
    | "t ∈ set (extra route ctx v)"
  using assms by (auto simp: routed_contribution_programs_def)

text ‹
  The local value a node's fold starts from: ‹bot0›, joined with the injected
  ‹d⇩0› at the CFG entry. An abbreviation, so every statement that spells the
  conditional out prints as ‹rhs_init› and needs no unfolding.
  ‹rhs_init_global› is the global counterpart: the injected ‹g⇩0› at the
  CFG entry, \<^const>‹bot› elsewhere.
›

abbreviation rhs_init :: "(var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ 'd::sup ⇒ 'd ⇒ pp ⇒ 'd" where
  "rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v ≡ (if v = cfg_entry g then bot0 ⊔ d⇩0 else bot0)"

abbreviation rhs_init_global :: "(var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ 'h::bot ⇒ pp ⇒ 'h" where
  "rhs_init_global g g⇩0 v ≡ (if v = cfg_entry g then g⇩0 else bot)"

definition routed_node_rhs ::
  "((var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ ((pp × 'c + 'k) × (var_id, exp) gedge_action) list)
   ⇒ ((var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ (pp × (var_id, exp) gcall_action) list)
   ⇒ ('c ⇒ 'k)
   ⇒ (pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c)
   ⇒ ('c ⇒ pp × 'c + 'k ⇒ (var_id, exp) gedge_action
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot,
                           'h::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) dg_state,
                          ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program)
   ⇒ ((pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ pp ⇒ pp
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program)
   ⇒ ((pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ pp
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program list)
   ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ 'd ⇒ 'd ⇒ 'h
   ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state) eqsT"
where
  "routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 =
     (λ(v, c).
        let t = sp_compile (side_rhs_fold_dg (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v)
                  (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g c v))
        in if v = cfg_entry g then Side (buffer_key_at c) (DG bot g⇩0) t else t)"

lemma eq_routed_node_rhs:
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb

      (v, ctx) τ =

     (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v)) bot"

        traverse_side_rhs_fold_dg[OF wf])

lemma sides_routed_node_rhs:
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb
                     extra g ctx w). sp_wf p"
  shows
  "sides_of_rhs (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0
      (v, ctx)) τ (Inr (buffer_key_at ctx)) =
   (if v = cfg_entry g then DG bot g⇩0 else bot)
   ⊔ (⨆t←routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v.
         sides_of_program t τ (Inr (buffer_key_at ctx)))"
  by (simp add: routed_node_rhs_def Let_def
        sides_of_program_side_rhs_fold_dg_char[OF wf]
        bot_dg_state_def[symmetric] ac_simps)

subsection ‹The right-hand side as a join of its parts›

text ‹
  At a fixed valuation the answer of \<^const>‹routed_node_rhs› is a join: the
  node's initial value, one value per intra edge entering it, one per call
  returning at it, and the values of the routing protocol's own programs, the
  seed read among them. \<^const>‹rhs_init› names the first part in the
  generator itself; the definitions below name the other three, and
  ‹routed_node_rhs_parts› states the answer as the join of all four. Every
  part is the local half of a contribution's answer at the valuation;
  publications do not enter the answer and are characterized by
  ‹sides_routed_node_rhs›.
›

definition rhs_edge where
  "rhs_edge it τ ctx u a = dg_local (traverse_program (it ctx u a) τ)"

definition rhs_call where
  "rhs_call cmb route τ ctx u ca v = dg_local (traverse_program (cmb route ctx ca u v) τ)"

definition rhs_seed where
  "rhs_seed extra route τ ctx v = (⨆p←extra route ctx v. dg_local (traverse_program p τ))"

lemma routed_node_rhs_parts:
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb
                     extra g c w). sp_wf p"
  shows
  "eq (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0)
      (v, c) τ =
   DG (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v
       ⊔ (⨆(u, a)←pred_sel g v c. rhs_edge it τ c u a)
       ⊔ (⨆(u, ca)←site_sel g v. rhs_call cmb route τ c u ca v)
       ⊔ rhs_seed extra route τ c v) bot"
  by (simp add: eq_routed_node_rhs[OF wf] side_acc_dg_as_foldr routed_contribution_programs_def
        rhs_edge_def rhs_call_def rhs_seed_def split_def comp_def sup_assoc)

subsection ‹Buffered generator: fold Side-free contributions, publish once›

text ‹
  \<^const>‹routed_node_rhs› writes a node's own key once per contribution
  that publishes there: several intra predecessors or several return call actions can
  each independently emit a ‹Side (buffer_key_at c) ...›, so several writes to the same
  ‹buffer_key_at c› land in one RHS evaluation. ‹routed_node_rhs_buffered› folds
  Side-free contribution programs (the caller's own ‹it_c› for ‹intra› and ‹cmb_c› for
  ‹comb›) with \<^const>‹fold_rhs_program› and publishes ‹buffer_key_at c› once, after every
  contribution has been read.

  That fold shapes the node's ∗‹own› key, but each call returning at the node
  still publishes its callee's seed from inside its ‹cmb_c› program. Two call sites
  resuming at the same node, as the two branches of a conditional that both end in a call
  do, can therefore name one seed key twice. \<^const>‹buffer_sides› accumulates the
  writes per key and preserves the declarative
  \<^const>‹traverse_rhs›/\<^const>‹sides_of_rhs› value wherever it flushes, so the
  correspondence with the unbuffered generator below is unaffected by the choice of
  flush points.

  The choice does decide when a seed reaches the solver. A call program publishes the
  seed and then reads the callee's exit, and that read is what makes the solver evaluate
  the callee. Held back to the answer, the seed arrives after the callee has been solved
  from \<^const>‹bot›, and the seed's change then sends the caller round again. So a node
  at which at most one call returns flushes before every local read, and the seed is
  published before the exit is read, in the order of Goblint's ‹sidel› and
  ‹getl›. A node at which several calls return flushes at the answer only: a flush
  before the first call's exit read would write a seed the second call may write again,
  and an update rule that keeps one slot per origin would see a partial contribution
  first in every evaluation. There ‹distinct_side_path_buffer_sides_answers_only›
  bounds the writes unconditionally; at the other nodes the bound
  ‹distinct_side_path_buffer_sides› holds as long as one call program does not
  write a seed again after reading an exit, which the shipped specifications'
  single-alternative entry transfers ensure.
›

definition routed_node_rhs_buffered ::
  "((var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ ((pp × 'c + 'k) × (var_id, exp) gedge_action) list)
   ⇒ ((var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ pp ⇒ (pp × (var_id, exp) gcall_action) list)
   ⇒ ('c ⇒ 'k)
   ⇒ (pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c)
   ⇒ ('c ⇒ pp × 'c + 'k ⇒ (var_id, exp) gedge_action
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot,
                           'h::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) dg_state,
                          ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program)
   ⇒ ((pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ pp ⇒ pp
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program)
   ⇒ ((pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ pp
        ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state, ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program list)
   ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcfg ⇒ 'd ⇒ 'd ⇒ 'h
   ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d, 'h) dg_state) eqsT"
where
  "routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it_c cmb_c extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 =
     (λ(v, c).
        let acc0 = DG (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v) (rhs_init_global g g⇩0 v);
            t = sp_compile (fold_rhs_program acc0
                  (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c extra g c v))
        in buffer_sides (λ_. length (site_sel g v) ≤ 1) (sp_lift_tree t (λres.
          Side (buffer_key_at c) (DG bot (dg_global res)) (Answer (DG (dg_local res) bot)))))"

text ‹
  \<^const>‹fold_rhs_program› threads its accumulator in evaluation order (a left
  fold); the lemmas below need the equivalent right fold, since that is how each of
  their own goals is already phrased. \<^const>‹traverse_rhs› only ever joins, so the
  two forms agree by ‹foldr_sup_seed_swap›.
›

lemma eq_routed_node_rhs_buffered:
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c
                     extra g ctx w). sp_wf p"
  shows
  "eq (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it_c cmb_c extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0)
      (v, ctx) τ =
   DG (dg_local (foldr (λt acc'. traverse_program t τ ⊔ acc')
     (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c extra g ctx v)
     (DG (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v) (rhs_init_global g g⇩0 v)))) bot"
  by (simp add: routed_node_rhs_buffered_def Let_def
        traverse_fold_rhs_program_char_foldr[OF wf])

text ‹
  The buffered generator answers the same join of parts as the reference
  generator, over its own contribution programs. Its answer carries no global
  half; the global part of the initial accumulator leaves through the
  publication at ‹buffer_key_at c›.
›

lemma routed_node_rhs_buffered_parts:
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb
                     extra g c w). sp_wf p"
  shows
  "eq (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0)
      (v, c) τ =
   DG (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v
       ⊔ (⨆(u, a)←pred_sel g v c. rhs_edge it τ c u a)
       ⊔ (⨆(u, ca)←site_sel g v. rhs_call cmb route τ c u ca v)
       ⊔ rhs_seed extra route τ c v) bot"
proof -
  have "eq (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0)
      (v, c) τ
    = eq (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0) (v, c) τ"
    by (simp add: eq_routed_node_rhs_buffered[OF wf] eq_routed_node_rhs[OF wf]
        dg_local_foldr_generic side_acc_dg_as_foldr_seeded)
  then show ?thesis
    by (simp only: routed_node_rhs_parts[OF wf])
qed

lemma sides_routed_node_rhs_buffered:
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c
                     extra g ctx w). sp_wf p"
    and intra_free_at_key:
      "⋀c' src a σ. sides_of_program (it_c c' src a) σ (Inr (buffer_key_at c')) = bot"
    and comb_free_at_key: "⋀c' ca cc ex σ.
                     sides_of_program (cmb_c route c' ca cc ex) σ (Inr (buffer_key_at c')) = bot"
    and extra_free: "⋀c' w σ z x. x ∈ set (extra route c' w) ⟹ sides_of_program x σ z = bot"
  shows "sides_of_rhs (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it_c cmb_c extra g
      bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 (v, ctx)) τ (Inr (buffer_key_at ctx)) =
   DG bot (dg_global (foldr (λt acc'. traverse_program t τ ⊔ acc')
     (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c extra g ctx v)
     (DG (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v) (rhs_init_global g g⇩0 v))))"
proof -
  have free: "⋀w σ x.
      x ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c extra g ctx w)
      ⟹ sides_of_program x σ (Inr (buffer_key_at ctx)) = bot"
    using intra_free_at_key comb_free_at_key extra_free
    by (blast elim: routed_contribution_programsE)
  show ?thesis
  proof (cases "v = cfg_entry g")
    case True
    have z0: "(sides_of_program (fold_rhs_program (DG (bot0 ⊔ d⇩0) g⇩0)
        (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it_c cmb_c extra g ctx
           (cfg_entry g))) τ) (Inr (buffer_key_at ctx)) = bot"
      by (simp only: sides_of_program_fold_rhs_program_char[OF wf]
          foldr_sup_bot_of_all_bot[OF free[where w = "cfg_entry g"]])

    and comb_s: "⋀c' ca cc ex τ. dg_global (traverse_program (cmb_c route c' ca cc ex) τ)
                     = dg_global (sides_of_program (cmb route c' ca cc ex) τ (Inr (buffer_key_at c')))"
    and comb_free_at_key: "⋀c' ca cc ex τ.
                     sides_of_program (cmb_c route c' ca cc ex) τ (Inr (buffer_key_at c')) = bot"
    and comb_sides_off_key: "⋀c' ca cc ex τ z. z ≠ Inr (buffer_key_at c')
                     ⟹ sides_of_program (cmb_c route c' ca cc ex) τ z
                         = sides_of_program (cmb route c' ca cc ex) τ z"
    and comb_dep: "⋀c' ca cc ex τ. dep_program τ (cmb_c route c' ca cc ex)
                     = dep_program τ (cmb route c' ca cc ex)"
    and extra_free: "⋀c' w τ z x. x ∈ set (extra route c' w) ⟹ sides_of_program x τ z = bot"
    and extra_local_only: "⋀c' w τ x.
                             x ∈ set (extra route c' w) ⟹ dg_global (traverse_program x τ) = bot"
    and pp_buf: "part_post_solution
        (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it_c cmb_c extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0)
        x sigma vars"
  shows "part_post_solution
      (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0) x sigma vars"
proof -
  let ?Tbuf =
    "routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it_c cmb_c extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0"
  let ?Told =
    "routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0"
  have corr: "⋀u. traverse_rhs (?Tbuf u) sigma = traverse_rhs (?Told u) sigma
      ∧ dep_aux sigma (?Tbuf u) = dep_aux sigma (?Told u)
      ∧ sides_of_rhs (?Tbuf u) sigma = sides_of_rhs (?Told u) sigma"
  proof -
    fix u :: "pp × 'c"
    obtain v ctx where u: "u = (v, ctx)" by (cases u) auto
    show "traverse_rhs (?Tbuf u) sigma = traverse_rhs (?Told u) sigma
        ∧ dep_aux sigma (?Tbuf u) = dep_aux sigma (?Told u)
        ∧ sides_of_rhs (?Tbuf u) sigma = sides_of_rhs (?Told u) sigma"
      unfolding u
      using routed_node_rhs_buffered_correspondence
              [where cmb = cmb and cmb_c = cmb_c and it = it and it_c = it_c
                 and route = route and buffer_key_at = buffer_key_at,
               OF wf_c wf
                  intra_t intra_side_pure intra_s intra_free_at_key intra_sides_off_key intra_dep
                  comb_t comb_side_pure comb_s comb_free_at_key comb_sides_off_key comb_dep
                  extra_free extra_local_only]
      by simp
  qed
  have "part_post_solution ?Tbuf x sigma vars = part_post_solution ?Told x sigma vars"
    by (rule part_post_solution_cong) (use corr in blast)+
  with pp_buf show ?thesis by simp
qed

  answer nothing on their global half. Such a program may still publish at
  ‹buffer_key_at ctx›: the buffered generator keeps that publication as it is
  and adds only the seed it hoists, which is the unbuffered generator's entry
  publication. A specification that splits its state and publishes the global
  half through its manager is of this kind.

lemma routed_node_rhs_buffered_correspondence_answers_local:
  fixes cmb ::
    "(pp ⇒ 'c ⇒ 'd ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ 'c) ⇒ 'c ⇒ (var_id, exp) gcall_action ⇒ pp ⇒ pp
       ⇒ (pp × 'c, 'k, ('d::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot,
                          'h::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) dg_state,
            ('d, 'h) dg_state) strategy_program"
  assumes wf: "⋀w. ∀p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb
                   extra g ctx w). sp_wf p"
    and answers_local: "⋀w p τ. p ∈ set (routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb
                   extra g ctx w) ⟹ dg_global (traverse_program p τ) = bot"
  shows "traverse_rhs
           (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0
             (v, ctx)) τ
         = traverse_rhs
           (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 (v, ctx)) τ"
    (is ?T)
    and "dep_aux τ
           (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0
             (v, ctx))
         = dep_aux τ
           (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 (v, ctx))"
    (is ?D)
    and "sides_of_rhs
           (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0
             (v, ctx)) τ
         = sides_of_rhs
           (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 (v, ctx)) τ"
    (is ?S)
proof -
  let ?ps = "routed_contribution_programs pred_sel site_sel route it cmb extra g ctx v"
  let ?acc0 = "DG (rhs_init g bot0 d⇩0 v) (rhs_init_global g g⇩0 v)"
  have glob: "dg_global (foldr (λt acc'. traverse_program t τ ⊔ acc') ?ps ?acc0)
      = dg_global ?acc0"
  proof -
    have "(⨆t←?ps. dg_global (traverse_program t τ)) = bot"
      by (rule foldr_sup_bot_of_all_bot) (rule answers_local)
    then show ?thesis
      by (simp only: dg_global_foldr_generic foldr_join_seed_out[where a = "dg_global ?acc0"]
          sup_bot_right)
  qed
  show ?T
  proof -
    have "traverse_rhs
        (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0
          (v, ctx)) τ
        = DG (foldr (λt acc'. dg_local (traverse_program t τ) ⊔ acc') ?ps (dg_local ?acc0)) bot"
      by (simp add: eq_routed_node_rhs_buffered[OF wf] dg_local_foldr_generic)
    also have "… = DG (side_acc_dg (dg_local ?acc0) τ ?ps) bot"
      by (simp add: side_acc_dg_as_foldr_seeded)
    also have "… = traverse_rhs
        (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 (v, ctx)) τ"
      by (simp add: eq_routed_node_rhs[OF wf])
    finally show ?thesis .
  qed
  show ?D
    by (simp add: routed_node_rhs_buffered_def routed_node_rhs_def Let_def
        dep_program_fold_rhs_program_char[OF wf] dep_program_side_rhs_fold_dg_char[OF wf])
  show ?S
  proof (rule ext)
    fix z
    show "sides_of_rhs
        (routed_node_rhs_buffered pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0
          (v, ctx)) τ z
      = sides_of_rhs
        (routed_node_rhs pred_sel site_sel buffer_key_at route it cmb extra g bot0 d⇩0 g⇩0 (v, ctx)) τ z"
      using glob
      by (cases "v = cfg_entry g")
         (auto simp: routed_node_rhs_buffered_def routed_node_rhs_def Let_def
            sides_of_program_fold_rhs_program_char[OF wf]
            sides_of_program_side_rhs_fold_dg_char[OF wf]
            traverse_fold_rhs_program_char_foldr[OF wf] sup_commute bot_dg_state_def[symmetric])
  qed
qed

subsection ‹Initial global values as side effects›

text ‹
  We represent an initial global value as an actual contribution to its global
  unknown, so the solved unknown itself contains that value. Reads do not need
  to join initialization into the stored value implicitly.
  ‹with_init init T› provides this for any equation system: the right-hand side of an
  unknown ‹x› first publishes every pair of ‹init x›, then continues as ‹T x›. An
  instance that publishes no initial values passes ‹λ_. []›, and the system is ‹T›.
›

definition init_sides :: "('g × 'd) list ⇒ ('x, 'g, 'd) strategy_tree ⇒ ('x, 'g, 'd) strategy_tree"
where
  "init_sides xs t = foldr (λ(k, d) t. Side k d t) xs t"

definition with_init :: "('x ⇒ ('g × 'd) list) ⇒ ('x, 'g, 'd) eqsT ⇒ ('x, 'g, 'd) eqsT"
where
  "with_init init T = (λx. init_sides (init x) (T x))"

lemma with_init_Nil [simp]: "with_init (λ_. []) T = T"
  by (simp add: with_init_def init_sides_def)

lemma init_sides_Cons [simp]: "init_sides ((k, d) # xs) t = Side k d (init_sides xs t)"
  by (simp add: init_sides_def)

lemma init_sides_Nil [simp]: "init_sides [] t = t"
  by (simp add: init_sides_def)

lemma traverse_rhs_init_sides [simp]: "traverse_rhs (init_sides xs t) σ = traverse_rhs t σ"
proof (induction xs)
  case (Cons p xs) then show ?case by (cases p) simp
qed simp

lemma dep_aux_init_sides [simp]: "dep_aux σ (init_sides xs t) = dep_aux σ t"
proof (induction xs)
  case (Cons p xs) then show ?case by (cases p) simp
qed simp

lemma sides_of_rhs_init_sides_ge:
  "sides_of_rhs t σ ≤ sides_of_rhs (init_sides xs (t :: ('x, 'g, 'd::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) strategy_tree)) σ"
proof (induction xs)
  case (Cons p xs)
  obtain k d where p: "p = (k, d)" by (cases p)
  have "sides_of_rhs (init_sides xs t) σ
          ≤ sides_of_rhs (init_sides (p # xs) t) σ"
    unfolding p le_fun_def by (simp add: Let_def)
  with Cons.IH show ?case by (rule order_trans)
qed simp

lemma sides_of_rhs_init_sides_at:
  "(k, d) ∈ set xs
   ⟹ d ≤ sides_of_rhs (init_sides xs (t :: ('x, 'g, 'd::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) strategy_tree)) σ (Inr k)"
proof (induction xs)
  case (Cons p xs)
  obtain k' d' where p: "p = (k', d')" by (cases p)
  show ?case
  proof (cases "(k, d) = p")
    case True
    with p show ?thesis by (simp add: Let_def)
  next
    case False
    with Cons.prems have "d ≤ sides_of_rhs (init_sides xs t) σ (Inr k)"
      by (intro Cons.IH) simp
    also have "… ≤ sides_of_rhs (init_sides (p # xs) t) σ (Inr k)"
      unfolding p by (simp add: Let_def)
    finally show ?thesis .
  qed
qed simp

text ‹
  A post-solution of the wrapped system bounds the wrapped system's own equations, since
  the wrapper only adds side effects, and covers every initial value at its key.
›

lemma tree_covered_at_init_sides:
  "tree_covered_at (init_sides xs (t :: ('x, 'g, 'd::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) strategy_tree)) σ u
   ⟹ tree_covered_at t σ u"
  unfolding tree_covered_at_def
  by simp (meson order_trans sides_of_rhs_init_sides_ge)

lemma dep_with_init [simp]: "dep (with_init init T) σ x = dep T σ x"
  by (simp add: dep_def with_init_def)

lemma post_bounded_with_init_at:
  assumes
    "post_bounded (with_init init (T :: ('x, 'g, 'd::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) eqsT)) x σ vars"
    and "u ∈ vars" and "(k, d) ∈ set (init u)"
  shows "d ≤ σ (Inr k)"
proof -
  have "sides_of_rhs (init_sides (init u) (T u)) σ ≤ σ"
    using post_boundedD[OF assms(1,2)] by (simp add: with_init_def tree_covered_at_def)
  then have "sides_of_rhs (init_sides (init u) (T u)) σ (Inr k) ≤ σ (Inr k)"
    by (rule le_funD)
  with sides_of_rhs_init_sides_at[OF assms(3)] show ?thesis by (rule order_trans)
qed

lemma part_post_solution_with_init:
  "part_post_solution (with_init init (T :: ('x, 'g, 'd::bounded_semilattice_sup_bot) eqsT)) x σ vars
   ⟹ part_post_solution T x σ vars"
  unfolding part_post_solution_iff_tree_covered_at
proof (intro conjI ballI; elim conjE)
  assume all: "∀u∈vars. dep⇩L (with_init init T) σ u ⊆ vars
                 ∧ tree_covered_at (with_init init T u) σ u"
  fix u assume u: "u ∈ vars"
  show "dep⇩L T σ u ⊆ vars" using all u by (simp add: dep⇩L_def)
  show "tree_covered_at (T u) σ u"
    using all u by (simp add: with_init_def) (blast intro: tree_covered_at_init_sides)
qed
