    carrier, normalization modulo a kind's width with Goblint's conversions, and an
    executed regression
Voblint_Analysis_Int

The int_dom analysis family: a reduced product of Sign, Interval, Parity and
    Congruence, with verified progressive and structural-fixpoint refinement, and the
    reduced product of Interval and Congruence at machine kinds, after Goblint's
    IntDomTuple
Voblint_Analysis_Interval

The Interval domain: widening and narrowing, arithmetic, numeric queries,
    kind-indexed operations with Goblint's normalization, and an executed regression
Voblint_Analysis_Order

The order relational domain over variable identities, run by the
    D/G pipeline over the elaborated CFG
Voblint_Analysis_Parity

The Parity domain: warrowing, arithmetic and comparison queries, and the
    domain at machine kinds
Voblint_Analysis_Sign

The Sign domain: warrowing, arithmetic, backward operators, and the domain at
    machine kinds
