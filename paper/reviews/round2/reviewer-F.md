# Reviewer F

## Summary

The paper connects a source-language semantics to a context-sensitive, side-effecting equation system and a previously verified solver. Activation traces retain the caller needed to interpret return flow; a relational context policy indexes their collecting semantics; five obligations connect that semantics to solved states. The endpoint concerns the exported analyzer function and definite verdicts, conditional on a returned report. I approached the submission looking for a fatal circularity or vacuity: entry-state contexts are defined using the very solution whose soundness is claimed. That rejection argument does **not** survive inspection. The context-free collecting semantics is fixed independently, totality prevents the policy from discarding executions, and a concrete source execution and informative returned report instantiate the endpoint. I found presentation and reproducibility problems, but no demonstrated false theorem, equivalent prior end-to-end result, or title/content mismatch.

## Recommendation

**Minor revision. Confidence: 4/5.** This assessment uses the frozen submission and targeted source inspection. I did not rebuild Isabelle. Public artifact completeness remains **UNVERIFIED**; a demonstrated missing proof dependency would require resolution before publication.

## Strengths

- The endpoint is stronger than an arbitrary-node existence claim: `src/Executable_Surface/CLI/Analysis_Certified.thy:196` proves existence of an observed node and coverage at every observed node. The observation relation is linked to source execution and compilation, rather than defined from the analyzer's answer.
- The non-vacuity evidence addresses the relevant logical objection. `src/Examples/Capstone/Example_Non_Vacuity.thy:151` combines an actual source execution, an `Analysed` report, two `PROVED` checks, and the endpoint's semantic conclusion. The source run is established at line 124; the analyzer computation is evaluated at line 44. This is an informative witness, not merely a satisfiable locale.
- The solver integration does real work beyond renaming a standard abstract-interpretation theorem. In particular, `src/Analyses/Shared/Result/DG_Live_Unknowns.thy:251` derives the forward coverage needed by the semantic argument from the backward dependency closure supplied by the solver.

## Major weaknesses

1. **The explanation of the solver bridge suppresses an important distinction between all solved unknowns and the subset used for coverage.** Section 4.3, p. 8, says that read closure makes all nodes of a procedure solved once its result is solved, then invokes `live_unknowns_cover` to obtain forward closure. The actual construction first restricts the solved set: `src/Analyses/Shared/Result/DG_Live_Unknowns.thy:230` defines `live_unknowns` by requiring that the enclosing procedure's result is also solved in that context. The source explanation at line 9 explicitly notes that the raw solved set can contain old queried keys and keys of procedures whose result was never solved. The theorem at line 251 concerns this restricted set. This is not a soundness defect, but it hides a key part of the claimed connection. Define the restricted set in the paper, distinguish it from the certificate's set, and explain why covering through that subset suffices for the published report. This needs a short mathematical explanation, not a catalogue of lemmas.

2. **The artifact statement contradicts itself at the point where independent verification matters.** Data availability, pp. 14–15, says that the vendored solver theories are archived on Zenodo, but also that the development “cannot yet be built from public sources alone” because their upstream repository is private. A private upstream does not prevent a build if the required vendored sources are in the public archive. Conversely, missing vendored theories would prevent reproduction of the central result. Section 4.3 explicitly relies on their certified-solver interpretation. State exactly what the archive contains, its immutable version, and which dependency—if any—is unavailable. I attempted the cited DOI through the web tool, which could not access it; this does **not** establish that the archive is absent. Public archive contents and a clean public-source build are **UNVERIFIED** in this review. This is a reporting defect with a potentially substantive consequence, not evidence that the local proof fails.

3. **The most suspicious-looking dependency deserves a more explicit argument.** Section 4.2, p. 8, states that the policy may depend on the analysis result without circularity because the final guarantee concerns the policy-independent collecting semantics. That conclusion is correct, but the explanatory step is compressed. `src/Abstract_Interpreter/Framework/Context/Routed_Context.thy:702` defines the policy from the solved valuation and jointly covering entry/resume alternatives; line 738 derives nonemptiness from `entry_pairs_cover`. The reader should see the quantifier order: fix a post-solution, construct its policy, establish totality and coverage for that policy, then forget the indices to recover the independently fixed collecting semantics. Otherwise the central contribution can initially look like choosing a specification to validate an answer. Add this chain and emphasize that arbitrary deletion of concrete calls is prohibited by Total. No new theorem is needed.

## Minor comments

1. Figure 2, p. 7: “Each box is proved without reference to the others” overstates independence. Routing adequacy explicitly mentions the analysis's `enter`, concretization, and solved valuation (`src/Abstract_Interpreter/Framework/Context/Routed_Context.thy:86`); totality is likewise solution-relative at line 104. The modules have separate obligations and compose through interfaces. Their proofs are not literally reference-free. Revise the caption accordingly.

2. Section 4.3, p. 8: distinguish syntactic graph reachability to a result node from feasible execution reaching that result. `live_unknowns_of_result` uses `local_reaches` (`src/Analyses/Shared/Result/DG_Live_Unknowns.thy:239`). Without this qualification, the sentence “Every node of a procedure reaches its result node” momentarily conflicts with the explicit coverage of nonterminating source executions.

3. Section 8, p. 14: the existing attribution to trace partitioning is appropriate and should remain explicit. The relevant prior conceptual machinery is already broad: Xavier Rival and Laurent Mauborgne, *The Trace Partitioning Abstract Domain*, ACM TOPLAS 29(5), article 26, 2007, DOI 10.1145/1275497.1275501, defines coverings and their semantic adequacy (Sections 3.2–3.3 of the [author manuscript](https://www.di.ens.fr/~rival/papers/toplas07.pdf)). Frédéric Dabrowski and David Pichardie, *A Certified Data Race Analysis for a Java-like Language*, TPHOLs 2009, LNCS 5674, pp. 212–227, DOI 10.1007/978-3-642-03359-9_16, already instruments a mechanized semantics with contexts ([author manuscript](https://davidpichardie.github.io/papers/tphols09.pdf), Sections 3–4). Both are already cited; I am not alleging missing related work. The defensible novelty lies in the particular mechanized connection to the executable side-effecting solver, not in assigning concrete meaning to contexts in general. I found no verified prior result that eliminates that contribution.

## Questions for author

1. Does the public Zenodo archive contain every vendored theory required for a clean build? If so, why does the availability statement say a public-source build is impossible?
2. Can Section 4.3 explicitly distinguish the solver's solved set, the live subset used in the coverage proof, and the set ultimately published in the report?
3. Can Section 4.2 display the solution-dependent policy and its totality argument in a few mathematical lines?

The strongest rejection argument was circular self-validation through solution-dependent contexts. It fails because the source semantics and unindexed collection remain fixed, the policy must retain every covered call, and the theorem is instantiated on an informative execution. Standard trace-partitioning precedents weaken a broad conceptual novelty claim, but the paper already credits them and claims the narrower verified pipeline. Neither argument supports rejection on the evidence inspected.
