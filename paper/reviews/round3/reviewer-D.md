# Reviewer D — formal-methods generalist

## Summary

The paper connects a source semantics for a scalar imperative language with recursive procedures to a context-sensitive, side-effecting equation system and a previously verified solver. Its principal intermediate result is a five-obligation coverage contract for activation traces, including a totality obligation for relational context policies. The exported analyzer's theorem covers stores and verdicts per program point; certification of individual context rows is explicitly excluded. The motivation and overall proof chain are understandable, but the equation-to-cover and source-to-graph interfaces need more mathematical detail for a self-contained journal article. This review uses only the current submission, with visual inspection of PDF pages 3, 10, and 11. Formalization correctness, literature accuracy, and benchmark reproducibility are **UNVERIFIED** independently.

## Recommendation

**Major revision. Confidence: 4/5.** The recommendation concerns journal self-containment and reproducibility, not an identified false theorem.

## Strengths

- Section 3.3, p. 6, states all five coverage obligations and explains the precise failure excluded by Total: Call and Return can otherwise hold vacuously while a resumed caller escapes the cover. The proof sketch identifies the caller-chain invariant and explains both uses of totality.
- Section 4.3, p. 9, identifies a substantive composition problem: the solver certificate is closed under backward reads, whereas semantic coverage requires forward closure. The explanation of structural paths to procedure results and the live subset gives a concrete reason the bridge can work.
- Section 5, pp. 10–11, distinguishes per-node coverage, per-context output, conditional verdicts, reachability, executable termination, and the trusted tracing hook. In particular, “No theorem about run_voblint certifies the row of an individual context” is an explicit and useful boundary.
- Figure 1, p. 3, gives visible call/return routing, a runnable-looking example, and the two concrete checks that motivate contexts. Its arrows and node labels are legible in the inspected rendering.

## Major weaknesses

1. **The central equation-to-cover connection is stated without displaying the equations needed to assess it.** Section 4.2, p. 7, says the right-hand side “joins four sources,” then describes routing and publication verbally. Theorem 2 and Table 1, p. 8, depend on `post_bounded`, forward closure, side effects, and seed separation, but the reader never sees a call-continuation right-hand side or a seed inequality. Section 4.1, p. 7, also introduces “two combine operations” and explains only one conceptual operation. Consequently, the sentence “adequacy with combine soundness gives Return” on p. 8 cannot be checked against a displayed construction, especially when enter supplies multiple pairs. Add a compact mathematical definition of the call contribution, its reads and seed writes, and the entry equation, then work through the Call/Return obligations against these definitions. This would expose the main new composition argument without reproducing Isabelle scripts.

2. **The endpoint theorem relies on source-language and observation interfaces that remain too implicit.** Section 5, p. 9, describes `pstep` as operating on “a residual command, a store, and a frame stack,” and says that `at_node` “observes a configuration at the nodes of its lifted runs.” Neither the frame contents nor a defining observation rule is given. Section 3.1, pp. 4–5, states the source-to-trace connection, but source call/return behavior is also only prose. Thus a reader can understand Theorem 3's quantifiers without assessing what counts as the same source state, or exactly which nodes a residual configuration may denote. Include a compact language/semantic interface, representative call and return rules, the well-formedness conditions relevant to unique calls, and one worked `at_node` example for the repeated-command case on p. 9. The unusual semantic choices currently postponed to p. 11 should be introduced alongside that interface. A full language reference is unnecessary; these are the definitions governing the theorem's meaning.

3. **The reviewable artifact does not yet support independent checking of the main mechanization claim.** The data-availability paragraph, Section 9, p. 16, states both that the archive “predates the context-policy claims and the profiling data used here” and that the vendored solver “is not yet public, so the theories cannot yet be built from public sources alone.” This is especially material because Section 6, p. 11, reports four local changes to that solver, including “a removed assumption,” and the principal result composes its certificate with the new development. A final-version promise does not let a reviewer establish that the submitted theorem chain builds. Supply a complete, versioned review artifact containing the actual solver dependency, modifications, and current generated evidence, with a documented build command. Whether such a privately reviewable artifact exists is **UNVERIFIED**; none is identified in the paper.

## Minor comments

1. **Terms used before definition or left insufficiently explained — explicit list.** “post-solution” (Introduction, p. 1) first receives an operational explanation through the certificate on pp. 8–9; give the inequality meaning at first use. “warrowing” (Figure 1 caption, p. 3) is explained only on p. 9. “locale” (`analysis_contract`, Section 4.1, p. 7) and “locale interpretation” (Section 6, p. 12) need a one-sentence gloss for readers outside Isabelle. “D/G framework” (Table 2, p. 12) has no expansion of D/G. “Int” (Section 7, p. 13) is used as the best configuration without explicitly connecting the name to the reduced product mentioned on p. 7. “placement of program globals” (Section 5, pp. 9–10) is listed as a configuration option without explaining its choices or meaning.

2. **Figure 2 has a visible collision.** On p. 11, the dashed `trace hook` box overlaps the label “proved in Isabelle/HOL.” Move the hook above the heading or move the heading left. Also clarify the caption's “Dashed boxes are handwritten code”: one dashed box contains “source text,” which is input rather than an implementation component. The theorem block on p. 10 has no comparable clipping in the inspected rendering.

3. **Figure 1 suppresses the value that explains combine.** At p. 3, both `exit_bump` boxes show only `n`, while the arrows lead to caller results 6 and 5. Add the abstract return value, or state where it resides, so the reader sees what combine imports from the callee. This is more informative than repeating the unchanged parameter interval.

4. **The performance summary omits basic aggregation choices.** Section 7, p. 14, reports “three repetitions each,” percentiles over 5,535 cases, and “21 cases” exceeding 30 seconds. Specify whether percentiles use per-case medians or all observations, whether timeouts are excluded, and how repeated timeouts count. The fitted exponents 2.01–2.86 also need the generated families and size ranges, at least in a small table. The text appropriately avoids claiming an asymptotic bound, but the measurements are not interpretable enough as presented.

5. **The external precision benchmark is too compressed to assess.** Section 7, pp. 13–14, reports eight checks from programs “VIMP can express,” five proved, and three requiring partitioning or widening changes. List the selected examples and their verdicts, including the reason each remaining example is excluded or fails. The pointer to the thesis does not replace that evidence within a journal article. The asserted diagnoses are **UNVERIFIED** from the paper alone.

6. **The memory limitation is broader than its stated rationale establishes.** Section 9, p. 16, says “Pointers into the caller's frame, a shared heap, or threads break this assumption,” referring to preservation of caller locals. Pointers into a caller's frame directly explain that failure. A shared heap's mere presence does not explain a change to caller locals; the sentence needs to specify the intended representation and aliasing assumption. Distinguish required extensions to the state/transfer interface from changes forced in activation-trace structure. Whether the existing development can support either extension is **UNVERIFIED**.

7. **Submission metadata still signals a conference template.** Page 1 prints ITP 2027 and placeholder editors “John Q. Open and Joan R. Access”; the footer repeats ITP throughout. For the requested journal assessment, replace these with appropriate submission metadata and remove the apparent publication DOI until assigned.

## Questions for the author

2. For `at_node` in Section 5, pp. 9–10, can one reached residual configuration be observed at multiple syntactically distinct nodes, and what invariant ensures that every such observation belongs to the same lifted source execution?
3. For the per-context gap acknowledged on p. 10, is the missing result only a report-construction lemma, or does report processing merge or relabel contexts in a way that requires additional assumptions?
4. Can reviewers obtain the complete solver version and current evidence named in the data-availability paragraph, p. 16, before acceptance?
