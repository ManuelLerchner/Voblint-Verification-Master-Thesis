## Formal work versus presentation and release work

**No new formal theorem is required by this decision.** R2 and R3 expose statements and constructions already present in the supplied development. They must preserve their assumptions; they should not change definitions to simplify the prose.

R1 requires release engineering and a future clean proof build of the distributed version. This review deliberately did not run a full Isabelle build. R4 requires data reduction and possibly small instrumented reruns, not new proofs. R5 requires wording and rendering checks.

A theorem certifying each individual context row would be new formal work and is optional (effort L until its proof obligations are scoped). First define row concretization and its relationship to report construction, then prove preservation of context indexing. Do not promise that it is merely bookkeeping without examining row merging, context conversion and serialization. A universal solver-termination theorem, general precision ordering, heap extension, or formal equivalence to Goblint would be a separate research project, outside this revision.
